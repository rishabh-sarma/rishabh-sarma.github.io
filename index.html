Welcome to my webpage! I am currently an S. Chowla Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at The Pennsylvania State University. I obtained my Ph.D. in Mathematics in August, 2025 from the University of Florida, where I was fortunate to be advised by Frank Garvan. In Fall 2023, I was a Chateaubriand Fellow at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), Université de Paris working with Jeremy Lovejoy.


My Ph.D. thesis is titled "Modular symmetries for rank-type partition statistics".


My area of research is additive number theory with a focus on integer partitions, q-series and modular forms. I use techniques from q-hypergeometric transformations, automorphic forms and combinatorics to look at problems in this area. I also employ automorphic-algorithmic approaches in Maple and Sage in my work.


Check out my Misc page for data on q-series computations using Maple, conferences in number theory, a compilation of postdoctoral opportunities in the US and abroad, and more.


I organize the Partitions/Combinatorics seminar at Penn State Math. It is held in a hybrid format via Zoom on Tuesdays at 11 am Eastern Time. Interested people are welcome to join at https://psu.zoom.us/j/96984458756.
